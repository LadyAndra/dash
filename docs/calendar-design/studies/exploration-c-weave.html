The month as fabric. No grid lines — weeks are woven bands, and how packed a band is, is the information: a quiet week is nearly bare cloth, a heavy week is dense with thread. Colour = project, fading toward cream as a date sits further out; ember = past its date. Weight shows as thickness. Demo is pinned to Aug 22.
